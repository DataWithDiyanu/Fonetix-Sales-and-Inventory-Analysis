# Fonetix Sales and Inventory Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing sales and inventory data from Fonetix, a retail business, to evaluate business performance, identify sales trends, understand product profitability and assess inventory management.

The project was undertaken as part of the Data for Impact Challenge using an original business dataset provided by Ismail, the owner of Fonetix.

The analysis covers a six-month period, from January to June 2026, with the aim of transforming business data into meaningful insights that can support informed decision-making.

## 3. Business Background

Fonetix is a retail business dealing in mobile phones, phone accessories, solar products, inverters, portable power products and other electronic appliances.

The business operates through two branches and maintains sales records, a product catalogue and inventory records.

## 4. Problem Statement

Retail businesses need reliable information about sales performance, product profitability and inventory movement to make informed business decisions.

This project analyses Fonetix's sales and inventory records to identify revenue drivers, evaluate product performance, compare branch sales and assess inventory accuracy.

The goal is to provide actionable, data-driven recommendations that can help management improve sales performance, profitability and inventory management.

## 5. Project Objectives

- Clean and prepare the original business dataset.
- Analyse overall sales and gross profit performance.
- Identify top-performing products and product categories.
- Examine monthly sales trends.
- Compare sales performance across branches.
- Evaluate product profitability.
- Validate inventory records.
- Develop actionable business recommendations.

## 6. Data Source

The dataset was provided by Ismail, the owner of Fonetix.

**Analysis Period:** January – June 2026.

**Dataset Components:**
- Original sales data.
- Product catalogue.
- Inventory data.

The dataset contains 3,600 sales transaction records, 32 products in the product catalogue and 384 inventory records.

## 8. Data Cleaning and Preparation

The original dataset was reviewed and prepared before analysis to improve data quality and ensure reliable results.

The data preparation process included:

- Checking for missing values.
- Checking for duplicate transaction IDs.
- Validating transaction dates.
- Checking quantity and unit price values.
- Validating product IDs against the product catalogue.
- Retrieving unit costs using lookup functions.
- Creating calculated columns for gross sales, net sales, month and gross profit.
- Reconciling inventory records against expected closing stock.

A separate Data Cleaning Log worksheet was created within the main Excel analysis workbook to document the quality checks, findings and actions taken.

## 9. Data Analysis

The analysis focused on the following areas:

1. Overall sales performance.
2. Monthly sales trends.
3. Sales performance by product category.
4. Top-performing products.
5. Branch performance comparison.
6. Gross profit and gross profit margin.
7. Inventory reconciliation.

Pivot Tables and charts were used to summarise the data and develop a sales dashboard.

## 10. Key Findings and Insights

The analysis revealed the following:

- **Total Net Sales:** Approximately ₦1.546 billion.
- **Gross Profit:** Approximately ₦209.21 million.
- **Gross Profit Margin:** 13.53%.
- **Total Transactions:** 3,600.
- **Total Units Sold:** 9,989.
- **Top Revenue Category:** Phones, contributing approximately 49.2% of total net sales.
- **Highest Monthly Sales:** June 2026.
- **Product Profitability:** Phone accessories recorded a higher gross profit margin than phones.
- **Inventory Validation:** All 384 inventory records reconciled with expected closing stock.

## 11. Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following recommendations were proposed:

- Monitor stock availability for high-performing products.
- Explore suitable phone and accessory bundles to improve profitability.
- Investigate monthly sales fluctuations.
- Compare product demand and performance across branches.
- Continue regular inventory reconciliation and physical stock verification.

## 12. Expected Business Impact

Implementing these recommendations could help Fonetix:

- Improve stock availability.
- Make better purchasing and replenishment decisions.
- Identify opportunities to improve gross profitability.
- Strengthen inventory monitoring.
- Improve sales planning.
- Support better business decision-making.

## 13. Project Limitations

- The analysis covers six months of business data.
- Operating expenses were not provided, so net profit could not be calculated.
- Marketing expenditure, supplier lead times and customer demographics were not available.
- Inventory reconciliation does not independently confirm physical stock quantities.
